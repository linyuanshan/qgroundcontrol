## Evidence Layout

Primary artifacts are retained under `build/P2-13-evidence/S01` through `S04` and are not committed. Each
scenario retains its v2 `.plan`, `.tlog`, planned-path screenshot, actual-trajectory screenshot, and any small
trajectory CSV used for distance analysis. SHA256 values are recorded below.

## Common Procedure

For every scenario:

1. Generate with `marine.coverage.bcd` and verify successful structural roles and metrics.
2. Audit every canonical path leg against `TrackFeasibleRegion` and rerun nominal coverage validation offline.
3. Save the v2 `.plan`, unload it, reload it, and confirm that planning does not rerun and that path, roles,
   metrics, selected angle, cell count, turn count, and No-Go geometry are unchanged.
4. Use the reloaded artifact to append and upload the mission. Confirm item count equals path-point count and all
   generated commands are `MAV_CMD_NAV_WAYPOINT` with no Marine-generated RTL, speed, camera, or sensor command.
5. Place the Rover at or safely near the first planned entry, arm, select AUTO, and observe every waypoint in
   order through `Mission Complete` at the final planned waypoint.
6. Retain telemetry and screenshots. Distinguish planned-centerline safety from vehicle tracking and corner
   cutting. Entry into an original No-Go polygon is a hard scenario failure.

Coverage and Transit roles are planning/visualization semantics only; P2 does not claim different execution-side
sensor behavior for the two roles.

## Environment and Known Limitations

- P2 guarantees the planned centerline lies in `TrackFeasibleRegion`; it does not implement turn-radius-aware or
  launch-to-first-entry routing.
- `WP_RADIUS`, vehicle tracking, and corner cutting can move the actual trajectory away from the planned polyline.
- As of protocol creation, the pre-SITL Debug build and all 25 Marine test classes pass. The Docker Desktop daemon
  must still be made available before execution evidence can be collected.
